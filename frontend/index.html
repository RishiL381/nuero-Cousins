    Method and references

    Baseline: low-demand "vanilla" task with eyes open (Jennings et al., 1992; Barry et al., 2007). Stressor: quick math in the style of the Montreal Imaging Stress Task (Dedovic et al., 2005), colour words (Stroop, 1935; Renaud & Blondin, 1997) or serial subtraction from the Trier Social Stress Test (Kirschbaum et al., 1993), all with MIST time pressure and social comparison; uncontrollable, socially evaluated tasks give the strongest stress responses (Dickerson & Kemeny, 2004). Self-report: six-item STAI (Marteau & Bekker, 1992). Signal selection: Cohen's d ≥ 0.5 (Cohen, 1988). EEG band power: Welch (1967); alpha and cognitive state: Klimesch (1999); Muse validity: Krigolson et al. (2017). Full list: docs/methods.md.
